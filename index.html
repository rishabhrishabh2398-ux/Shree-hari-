    Golden rules

    • Tally is the boss. Customers, items, stock, invoices and dues are only read from Tally.
    • Nothing is written to Tally without the owner's approval. Approved sales orders, challans (as Delivery Notes) and purchase orders go in through Tally's import, after a backup.
    • Stock changes only through Tally entries (delivery notes, invoices, purchases). Nobody types stock numbers.
    • Orders over a party's credit limit, or with bills older than 90 days, wait for the owner.
    • A challan can't send more than is free in stock.
    • Salesmen only see their own parties. Stock value and accounts are owner-only.
    • Every action is logged with who did it and when.
